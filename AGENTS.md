# AGENTS.md — credit-decisioning-e2e

Status: active · 2026-11-17 · Phase 1 (score) done; Phase 2 (scoring API) next.

This project is built with [data-science-harness](https://github.com/samuelkanneman/data-science-harness) (commit `644c4f2`), the author's method for doing data science with coding agents, and adopts its credit-risk profile. The harness is referenced, not copied: read its `docs/metodo.md` and `docs/perfiles/riesgo-crediticio/` for the rules summarised here.

## Goal and scope

An end-to-end credit decisioning system on public Lending Club data: scorecard, scoring API, rules engine and monitoring. The plan and the status of each phase are in `docs/roadmap.md`.

Out of scope: any data, rule or variable name from a real lender. Examples and test data are synthetic.

## Environment and sources

- Python 3.12, dependencies locked in `uv.lock`. Always run through `uv run` or `make`.
- Source: Kaggle dataset `wordsforthewise/lending-club` (CC0), snapshot of March 2019, pinned by SHA-256 in `data_manifest.json`.
- Kaggle credentials come from the environment and are never written to the repository. Data, models and MLflow runs are git-ignored.

## Flow

```text
make data      download, verify hashes, convert to parquet (reports rows dropped and values lost)
make audit     docs/data_audit.md
make dataset   population, target, split, features, contract check
make binning   docs/binning.md, models/binning.pkl
make train     docs/validation_report.md, models/scorecard-v1/, MLflow run
make sample    synthetic raw data instead of `make data`, for tests and CI
make lint test
```

Reports under `docs/` marked "Generated" are regenerated by these commands, never edited by hand.

## Data contract

- Unit and key: one loan, `id`, unique.
- Population: individual applications issued 2013–2017. Joint applications, earlier years and 2018 are excluded (ADR-002).
- Target: `bad_12m`, a proxy for 90+ days past due within 12 months of issue, defined in `src/credit_score/target.py`. Every cohort has at least 15 months of observation.
- Split by issue year: development 2013–2015, out-of-time 2016, simulated production 2017.
- Columns are read through the whitelist in `src/credit_score/data/schema.py`. Only `feature` columns may enter a model; pricing, Lending Club's grade and outcomes never do.
- The training population is loans Lending Club approved. Results do not extend to rejected applicants without the reject-inference work of Phase 3.

## Method rules that apply here

- Fit, selection and evaluation are separate. Bins, WoE, variable selection, coefficients and calibration use development only. Out-of-time is for the final evaluation and decides nothing before it.
- A model experiment is preregistered in `docs/experiments/` (hypothesis, baseline, success criteria) before it is trained.
- Metrics come from `src/credit_score/metrics.py` and follow the profile: AUC as a fraction with the score oriented so that higher is better, canonical KS by deciles in percent, decile 1 the riskiest, PSI against a fixed reference with bands 0.10 and 0.25. Any other variant is labelled as such.
- A move of more than 3 percentage points in AUC or KS against a comparable period needs a diagnosis (mix, policy, range restriction) before it is called model decay.
- Numeric conversions count the values they turn into missing. A missing target is never a good.
- States are named precisely: implemented, verified offline (synthetic data or tests), validated on the real data. A check that was not run is reported as pending.
