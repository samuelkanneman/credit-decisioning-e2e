# Policy analysis

Generated by `make policy-analysis`. Do not edit by hand.

## Contract

- **Question.** Where should the score cut-off sit, which knock-out rules earn their place, and how large a loan can each band carry?
- **Population.** Individual 36-month loans issued 2013–2015 that have run their full term: 545,779 loans, 14.07% charged off. This is the scorecard's development sample, so the figures are in-sample for the model; the 2017 simulation is the out-of-sample test of the policy.
- **Profit.** Cash received over the life of the loan, recoveries included, minus the amount lent and a funding and servicing cost of 6.5% of the amount. Measured, not modelled.
- **Stress.** 1.34 times as many charge-offs at every score, the rise observed in 12-month defaults between development and 2016.
- **Limits.** Applicants are loans Lending Club approved, priced by Lending Club's own grade. A margin here is a margin at those prices. 60-month loans are not in the economics because most have not matured.
